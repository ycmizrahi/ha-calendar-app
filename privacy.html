Privacy policy

Home Calendar Link connects the owner's Google Calendar to the owner's own Home Assistant installation at home.

  • What it accesses: the calendars of the Google account that signs in, to show and edit events on a home display.
  • Where data goes: only to the owner's Home Assistant, on the owner's own hardware. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Storage: the sign-in token is stored only in that Home Assistant installation.
  • Removing access: at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
